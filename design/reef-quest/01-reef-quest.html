            Reef Quest · design notes

            • One screen, one game. Chat history leaves the main view: the orchestrator talks to you in a game text box, and the full history lives in JOURNAL (alt+m). Tool output never floods the screen; it becomes one battle-text line in the LOG.
            • Thoughts are your choice. alt+t shows the orchestrator's reasoning in a dashed thought box over the world, or hides it behind a small "thinking" chip and the bubble over its head. The choice persists per session.
            • Status as game HUD. Context left is a row of coffee cups (Zelda hearts), cost is the gem counter, model and profile are the equipped B/A items, project and branch are the area name (big banner when you enter, small plate after).
            • Party, quest, save. Subagents join the party (Pokemon slots); the todo list is the current quest; SAVE is Engram memory; MAP is the Status card (changes, integrations, permissions, RDD).
            • The room. Same engine and reducer as the web office, story camera at 2x (about 12 rows in view), only the acting character animates. In the terminal the room is one Kitty image; HUD, panels and text are terminal cells.
            • Fallback. Without Kitty graphics the world row collapses; HUD, party, quest, log and dialog keep working as text.
